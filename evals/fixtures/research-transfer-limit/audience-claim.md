# Daily Harbor Cup — Audience Fit Memo

## Product direction

The game is a mobile asynchronous harbor builder intended for a broad relaxed-play audience. Players usually open it once or twice a day, rearrange docks, and trade cargo with friends. The team has not yet chosen premium purchase or free-to-play with optional purchases.

## Proposed event

Every day, all active players enter a 24-hour Harbor Cup. A global leaderboard ranks cargo value delivered during that window. The top 10% receive an exclusive harbor decoration and a seven-day production boost. Missing a day resets the player's event streak.

## Current claim

Daily competitive events are appropriate for the game's relaxed asynchronous mobile audience and will improve retention without making play feel pressured. The claim is intended to cover the broad target audience, both candidate business models, and the global leaderboard plus its proposed rewards and streak reset.

## Current evidence

No project-specific playtest, telemetry, audience survey, reward-economy analysis, or monetization decision has been completed. The feature is currently justified by the team's recollection that several successful mobile games run recurring competitions.
